Homie

One phone number the whole family can text. Homie turns the group-chat chaos into a daily plan.

I started with a PRD and spec, then built it in small slices I could test.

  • No new app, just a text. If you can text, you’re in.
  • AI organizes, the family decides. Every plan traces back to the texts it came from, and anyone can correct it.
  • Our chief of staff. The mental load gets shared instead of landing on one person, and one digest a day keeps everyone in the loop.
TypeScript · Postgres · Twilio · OpenAI structured outputs · Vercelcode
  1. Text itAnyone in the family texts one shared number.
  2. Save & sort itEach text lands in a private family timeline, and AI turns the day’s notes into plans, tasks and reminders.
  3. Send itA daily digest goes out by text.
